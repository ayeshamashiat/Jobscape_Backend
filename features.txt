# Jobscape - Implemented Features Documentation

Jobscape is a comprehensive job platform facilitating seamless interactions between Job Seekers and Employers. Below are the detailed features implemented in the system.

## 1. User Authentication & Security
*   **Dual-Role Registration**: Separate registration flows for Job Seekers (Basic) and Employers.
*   **Email Verification**: Token-based email verification using SendGrid/SMTP. Mandatory for account activation.
*   **Secure Login**: JWT-based authentication with role-specific payloads.
*   **Password Management**: Secure password hashing (bcrypt) and token-based "Forgot Password" reset flow.
*   **Rate Limiting**: Implementation of registration rate limiting to prevent spam and bot attacks.
*   **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Strict separation of Seeker and Employer functionalities using FastAPI dependencies.

## 2. Job Seeker Profile & Resume Management
*   **AI-Powered CV Parsing**: Automated extraction of text from uploaded resumes (PDF/DOCX) using AI to auto-populate profile fields (Skills, Experience, Education, Contact info).
*   **Cloudinary Integration**: Secure storage and retrieval of resume files.
*   **Profile Completion Tracking**: System tracks if a user has completed their profile (mandatory for job applications).
*   **Professional Profile**: Display of summary, skills, experience, projects, certifications, and social links (LinkedIn, GitHub, Portfolio).

## 3. Job Management (Employer Side)
*   **Job Lifecycle**: Create, update, delete, close, and reopen job listings.
*   **Subscription & Limits**: Job posting limits based on subscription tiers (Free, Pro, Enterprise) and verification status.
*   **Advanced Job Posting**: Define detailed requirements including salary ranges, work mode (Remote/On-site/Hybrid), job type, experience level, and industry.
*   **Match Preview**: Employers can see a "Match Score" preview for candidates.

## 4. Job Discovery & Application (Seeker Side)
*   **Advanced Search & Filtering**: Multi-parameter search by keyword, skills, location, salary range, company size, and verification tier.
*   **AI Match Scoring**: Real-time calculation of how well a seeker's profile matches a job description before they even apply.
*   **Seamless Application**: Single-click application using the primary resume with optional cover letter support.
*   **Application Tracking**: Dashboard to monitor real-time status (Pending, Shortlisted, Interviewing, Hired, Rejected).
*   **Application Withdrawal**: Option to withdraw applications before they are processed.

## 5. Application Management & ATS (Employer Side)
*   **Recruitment Dashboard**: Centralized view of all applications across all posted jobs.
*   **ATS Scoring (AI-Powered)**: Automated scoring of resumes against job descriptions, providing a detailed report and recommendation.
*   **Bulk Actions**:
    *   **Bulk ATS Scoring**: Score all pending applications at once.
    *   **Bulk Shortlisting**: Automatically shortlist or reject candidates based on a configurable ATS score threshold.
*   **Candidate Ranking**: Rank applicants by ATS score or match score for faster screening.

## 6. Interview System
*   **Hybrid Scheduling**:
    *   **Manual Scheduling**: Employer proposes multiple time slots; seeker picks one to confirm.
    *   **FCFS Slot Pooling**: Employer creates a pool of available slots; shortlisted candidates book them on a First-Come, First-Served basis.
*   **Interview Styles**: Support for Video Call, In-Person, Phone, and Other.
*   **Integrated Video Room**: Internal video conferencing integration where room IDs are automatically generated for confirmed interviews.
*   **Real-time Notifications**: Automated email and in-app notifications for invitations, confirmations, and "Interview Started" events.

## 7. Assessment & Evaluation
*   **Custom Assessments**: Employers can create job-specific quizzes/assessments (MCQ, True/False) that candidates must take after being shortlisted.
*   **Auto-Grading Logic**: System automatically grades assessments and records scores.
*   **Interview Reviews**: Structured evaluation forms for employers to rate candidates (1-5 stars) and add private metrics/notes after interviews.

## 8. Selection & Hiring
*   **Selection Process Builder**: Define custom recruitment rounds (e.g., Initial Screening, Technical Round, Final HR).
*   **Status Advancements**: Move candidates through defined rounds with automated notifications.
*   **Hiring Announcement**: Final selection flow to hire specific candidates, which automatically notifies and updates their profiles to "Employed," while gracefully rejecting other candidates.

## 9. Communication & Real-time Updates
*   **In-App Chat**: 
    *   Application-linked rooms for context-aware messaging.
    *   Direct Messaging for general inquiries.
    *   File Attachments in chat (Images, PDFs).
    *   Unread message tracking and total counts.
*   **Broadcast Messaging**: Bulk messaging capability to reach all candidates in a specific recruitment stage (e.g., notify all shortlisted candidates about a delay).
*   **Notification System**: Real-time alerts for all critical actions (New Application, Shortlist, Message, Interview Invite).

## 10. Miscellaneous Features
*   **Email Templates**: Rich HTML email notifications for premium user experience.
*   **Commute Integration**: Functionality to manage and display commute-related information (preliminary implementation).
*   **Admin Dashboard Features**: Backend routes for platform-wide management.
